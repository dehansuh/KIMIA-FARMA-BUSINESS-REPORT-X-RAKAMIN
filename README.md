Kimia Farma Business Performance Analysis (2020-2023)

Project Overview
This project evaluates the business performance of PT Kimia Farma Tbk, a leading state-owned pharmaceutical company in Indonesia, spanning the period from 2020 to 2023. By integrating various datasets including transactions, inventory, branch operations, and product catalogs, this analysis aims to uncover revenue trends, sales effectiveness, and branch performance to support data-driven business decisions.

Tools & Technologies
- Database & Data Processing: Google BigQuery (SQL)
- Data Visualization & Dashboarding: Google Looker Studio
- Techniques Used: Data Integration, Data Transformation, Performance Analytics, Business Intelligence

Key Insights & Business Impact
Based on the dashboard analytics, here are the high-level performance metrics achieved:
- Total Transactions: 672,458 transactions processed and analyzed.
- Total Revenue (Nett Sales): Rp 321,171,190,319 generated across all branches.
- Total Profit: Rp 91,214,988,060 secured.
- Top Performing Region: Jawa Barat (West Java) emerged as the highest contributing province, generating over Rp 26.9 Billion in net profit.

Repository Structure
